Dal Tadka

PunjabiVegServes 2

Dal tadka or Tadka Dal is one of the most basic yet one of the most popular dal recipes served in any Indian Restaurant. Lentils tempered with spices and ghee, this Dal Tadka is one of the most popular Indian dishes and also my personal favorite.

Ingredients

For Pressure Cooking

  • ½ cup toor dal
  • 2 tbsp masoor dal
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • salt to taste
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 1½ cup water

For Dal

  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • 1 tej patta
  • pinch hing
  • 2 dried red chilli
  • 2 clove garlic (crushed)
  • 1 inch ginger (crushed)
  • 1 chilli (slit)
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • ¼ tsp haldi
  • ½ tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • salt to taste
  • 1 tomato (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp coriander (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (crushed)
  • ¼ tsp garam masala

For Tadka

  • 1 tsp ghee
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • pinch hing
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • ¼ tsp kashmiri red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander (finely chopped)

Instructions

  1. firstly, in a pressure cooker take ½ cup toor dal, 2 tbsp masoor dal. you can also add moong dal if you prefer.
  2. also add ¼ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp oil and 1½ cup water.
  3. pressure cook for 5 whistles or until dal is cooked well.
  4. add 1 cup water and adjust consistency. keep aside.
  5. now in a large kadai heat 2 tbsp ghee and splutter ½ tsp cumin, 1 bay leaf, pinch hing and 2 dried red chilli. also add 2 clove garlic, 1 inch ginger, 1 chilli and saute slightly.
  6. further add 1 onion and saute until it turns golden brown.
  7. keeping the flame on low add ¼ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp coriander powder and ½ tsp salt.
  8. add 1 tomato and saute until it turns soft and mushy.
  9. add in cooked dal and stir well adjusting consistency as required.
  10. simmer for 5 minutes or until flavours are well combined.
  11. now add 2 tbsp coriander, 1 tsp kasuri methi and ¼ tsp garam masala. mix well.
  12. finally, enjoy dal tadka with jeera rice or steamed rice.
