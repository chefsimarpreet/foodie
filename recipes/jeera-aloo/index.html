Jeera Aloo

PunjabiVegServes 3

All time favorite Punjabi dish, best paired with poori.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • ¼ tsp Hing
  • 1 fresh Green Chilli
  • 1 sprig Curry leaves
  • 5 Potatoes (medium) – boiled & cubed
  • ¼ tsp Turmeric
  • ¼ tsp Red Chilli
  • ½ tsp Sugar
  • Salt to Taste
  • 1 tsp Lime Juice
  • Coriander leaves (Chopped)

Instructions

  1. Heat the wok, add Mustard Oil, Just make sure it reaches the smoking point and then put the tadka (cumin seeds, mustard seeds, hing, green chilli and curry leaves) in simmer… Careful not to burn it though.. You will see white smoke starting to slowly come out.. immediately sim at that point.
  2. Once the Cumin seeds and Rye begin to splutter, add the spices and salt.
  3. At This point add the boiled and cubed potatoes and saute. Add a tsp of Lime juice
  4. Garnish with Coriander leaves and lemon wedges.
  5. Serve hot with Pooris.
