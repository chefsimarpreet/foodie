Veggie Spring Roll

ChineseVegServes 3

In a mood for something fun and delicious ? Here's my recipe for Veg Spring roll. Do try :)

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic, chopped fine
  • 1 green chili, chopped fine
  • ½ cup carrot, shredded
  • ½ cup Cabbage, shredded
  • ¼ cup Capsicum, julienne
  • ¼ cup French bean, julienne
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ½ tsp light soy sauce

For Binding Flour

  • 2 tbsp refined flour (maida)
  • 1 tbsp water

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a wok on a medium flame, add ginger –garlic and sauté until raw smell goes off.
  2. Then add the chopped vegetables and saute lightly.
  3. After that add salt and black pepper. Saute until the vegetables are half done.
  4. Add red chili sauce and light soy sauce and cook for few more seconds. Keep aside.
  5. Defrost the spring roll sheets and keep them in damp cloth.
  6. Place a spoonful of filling in the center, fold the sides and roll it seal the ends.
  7. Make all the rolls in the same manner.
  8. Now heat oil in a fry in pan.
  9. Deep fry the spring rolls on medium high heat until golden brown.
  10. Serve hot with Schezwan Sauce.
