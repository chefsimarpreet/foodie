Pepper Chicken

IndianNon-vegServes 3

Pepper chicken is a hot and spicy side dish. It goes well with most of the main dishes and it is a perfect recipe for a Sunday Brunch.

Ingredients

  • 6 Boneless, Skinless Chicken Thighs
  • 1/2 Cup Hungcurd
  • 1/2 Tbsp Lemon
  • Salt and Pepper to taste
  • Lemon to Garnish
  • Olive Oil Cooking Spray

Instructions

  1. In a vessel, add the hungcurd, lemon, salt and pepper.
  2. Place the chicken in the vessel, Mix it well place it in the refrigerator for 7-8 hours.
  3. Once it’s fully coated, spray the basket of the air fryer with olive oil cooking spray.
  4. Pre heat the Air fryer at 200°C for 10-12 minutes.
  5. Put the chicken into the pre heated Air Fryer.
  6. Coat with olive oil cooking spray.
  7. Cook at 200°C for 20 minutes, after 10 minutes rotate the chicken thighs and spray with olive oil cooking spray.
  8. When done serve hot with plain rice, chapati or naan.
