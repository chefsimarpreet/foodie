Baingan Bharta

IndianVegServes 4

Smoky roasted eggplant mashed and simmered with onions, tomatoes and warm spices — a classic North Indian comfort dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium Brinjals
  • 10 garlic cloves (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup green coriander
  • 2 tbsp grated ginger
  • 4 tomatoes (finely diced)
  • 1 green chili pepper
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Roasting the Brinjals on a grill or stovetop: Make slits all over the brinjal skin and insert the crushed garlic inside each until it’s firmly embedded. Using a pair of tongs, carefully roast the brinjal on each side until the skin is charred and a knife inserted in the thickest part goes cleanly through.
  2. When the brinjals are cool enough to handle, peel off the charred skin if you roasted them over an open fire, or, if you baked them, scoop out the flesh with a spoon. Chop roughly. You can also mash the brinjal, but I prefer chopping for the best flavor. Set the brinjal aside.
  3. In a saucepan, heat the oil and add the cumin. Let the cumin darken slightly and then add the onions, green chili if using, and half the green coriander. Season with some salt. Saute over medium heat until the onions soften and start to brown.
  4. Add the ginger, coriander powder and turmeric. Mix well to toast the spices, for about a minute. Then add in the tomatoes. Mix, and let them cook another five minutes or so until pulpy. You don’t want to cook them for too long.
  5. Add the chopped or mashed brinjal and mix thoroughly until it disappears into the sauce. Add salt as necessary.
  6. Turn off the heat after five minutes. Garnish with green coriander and serve hot.
