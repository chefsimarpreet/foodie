Cold Coffee

IndianVegServes 1

A perfect milkshake for hot summer and an easy alternative to filter coffee or extra hot cappuccino.

Ingredients

For Cold Coffee

  • 1 tbsp instant coffee powder
  • 2 tbsp warm water
  • 1½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 6 cubes ice
  • whipped cream

For Chocolate Cold Coffee

  • 1 tbsp instant coffee powder
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp warm water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp chocolate sauce
  • sugar to taste
  • 6 cubes ice
  • chocolate sauce (for decoration)
  • whipped cream (for decoration)

Instructions

Iced Cold Coffee Preparation

  1. take the coffee into the blender.
  2. add 1½ cup milk, 1 tbsp sugar and 6 cubes ice.
  3. blend well until the milkshake turns frothy.
  4. pour the cold coffee into a tall glass and garnish with whipped cream and coffee powder.

Chocolate Cold Coffee Preparation

  1. firstly, in a small bowl take 1 tbsp instant coffee powder and 1 tbsp cocoa powder.
  2. dissolve in 2 tbsp warm water.
  3. take the choco-coffee decoction into the blender. and add 1 cup milk, 1 scoop vanilla ice cream and 2 tbsp chocolate sauce.
  4. also, add 1 tbsp sugar and 6 cubes ice.blend well until the milkshake turns frothy.
  5. pour the chocolate cold coffee into a tall glass.
  6. finally, enjoy chocolate cold coffee garnished with whipped cream and coffee powder.
