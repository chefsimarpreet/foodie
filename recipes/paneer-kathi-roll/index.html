Paneer Kathi Roll

IndianVegServes 2

Popular Indian street-food, Paneer Kathi rolls are warm, layered parathas filled with spicy paneer, mixed peppers and sweet caramelized onions. Whether you make it for a quick weeknight meal or a leisurely gathering, these kathi rolls are sure to be a hit! Do Try :)

Ingredients

For Paneer Stuffing

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup paneer (cubed)
  • 2 tsp oil
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp kashmiri red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ¼ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 cup tomato pulp
  • 2 tbsp water
  • salt to taste
  • ½ capsicum (sliced)
  • 2 tbsp coriander (finely chopped)

Masalas

  • ½ tsp kashmiri red chilli powder
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp chaat masala
  • ½ tsp amchur
  • salt to taste

Other Ingredients

  • 2 wrap
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise (eggless)
  • 2 tsp chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp cabbage (shredded)
  • 2 tbsp carrot (grated)
  • few onion (sliced)
  • 2 tsp lemon juice

Instructions

Stuffing Preparation

  1. firstly, in a kadai heat 1 tbsp butter and roast 1 cup cubed paneer.
  2. roast until paneer turns golden brown. keep aside.
  3. now add 2 tsp oil and saute 1 onion, 1 tsp ginger garlic paste.
  4. saute until onions turn olden brown.
  5. further, add ¼ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp coriander powder, ¼ tsp cumin powder and ½ tsp garam masala.
  6. saute on low flame until spices turn aromatic.
  7. now add 1 cup tomato pulp and saute well. to prepare tomato pulp, blend 3 ripened tomato to a smooth paste.
  8. saute until the oil separates from tomato paste.
  9. further, add 2 tbsp water, ½ tsp salt and mix well
  10. add in roasted paneer, ½ capsicum and mix well.
  11. cover and simmer for 4 minutes.
  12. now add 2 tbsp coriander and mix well. paneer stuffing ready.

Masala Preparation

  1. firstly, in a small bowl take ½ tsp chilli powder, ¼ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cumin powder, ½ tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp garam masala, ½ tsp chaat masala, ½ tsp amchur and ¼ tsp salt.
  2. mix well and spice mix is ready.

Paneer Kathi Roll Prepartrion

  1. firstly, take wrap or chapati and warp if required.
  2. add 1 tbsp eggless mayonnaise and 1 tsp chilli sauce. mix and spread uniformly.
  3. now add in 2 tbsp prepared paneer stuffing and spread slightly.
  4. top with 1 tbsp cabbage, 1 tbsp carrot and few onions. sprinkle in prepared frankie masala and squeeze 1 tsp lemon juice. now roll tight without allowing the stuffing to come out. tuck the ends make it well secured. finally, cut into halves and enjoy paneer roll.
