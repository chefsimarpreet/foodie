Simple Spinach Soup

IndianVegServes 2

Spinach Soup has a nutty flavour and rustic aroma which are the highlights of this recipe. It is made with very few ingredients and is also easy to prepare.

Ingredients

For Spinach Soup

  • 2 ½ cups Milk
  • 1 bunch chopped Spinach, washed and drained
  • 2 tbsp Salted Butter
  • 2 tbsp Corn flour
  • ½ cup finely chopped onions
  • Salt to taste

For Garnish

  • 2 tbsp Fresh Cream

Instructions

  1. Take a deep Pan, heat the butter, add the onions and saute on a medium flame for 1-2 minutes or till the onions are translucent.
  2. Add the spinach and saute on a medium flame for another 2 minutes.
  3. Add 2 cups of water, mix well and cook on a medium flame for 5 to 7 minutes, while stirring continuously.
  4. Allow to cool completely.
  5. Once cooled, blend it in a mixer into a smooth puree.
  6. Strain the puree with a help of a sieve and transfer it back into a deep pan.
  7. Combine the cornflour and milk mixture and combine making sure there are no lumps. Add it into the strained Spinach Puree and mix well.
  8. Cook on medium flame for another 2 minutes, while stirring occasionally.
  9. Add salt and pepper and cook for another 1 minute.
  10. Serve the hot soup garnished with fresh cream.
