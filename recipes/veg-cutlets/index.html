Veg Cutlets

IndianVegServes 2

Aloo tikki is a snack originating from the Indian subcontinent; in North Indian, Pakistani, and Bangladeshi preparation, it is made of boiled potatoes, peas, veggies and various curry spices.

Ingredients

  • Oil for shallow frying
  • Ginger Garlic finely chopped 1 tsp
  • Onion - 1 small (finely chopped)
  • Potato - 2 large
  • Green Peas - 1/4 cup
  • Corn Kernels - 1/4 cup
  • Mint - 1 sprig (finely chopped)
  • Coriander leaves - 1 tbsp (finely chopped)
  • Bread Slice - 2
  • Salt - to taste
  • Garam Masala -1 tsp
  • Red Chilli Powder - 1 tsp
  • Jeera Powder - 1 tsp
  • Green Chilli - 2 (finely chopped)

Instructions

  1. First cook the potatoes, mash them and keep aside.
  2. Boil the Peas and Corn Kernels, Chop them in the chopper.
  3. Now heat a pan with 1 tsp oil, add the ginger, garlic, onion and saute until the onion turns translucent. Turn off the flame.
  4. Now, to the sauteed onion mixture, add the mashed potatoes, cooked Peas and Corn Kernels, mint leaves, coriander leaves, green chillies, red chilli powder, jeera powder, garam masala powder, salt, amchur powder. Add Bread slice to the mixing bowl. Now, mix all the ingredients until well combined.
  5. Now heat a pan with oil for shallow frying and set the flame to Medium. While the oil is getting heated. Divide the mixture into equal sized balls according to your preference.
  6. Shallow fry the patties until both the sides of the cutlet turns golden brown by flipping. It took me about 2 mins on each side. Once done remove from the oil and place in a paper towel to remove excess oil. Repeat the same with the other cutlets.
  7. Enjoy hot with Green Coriander Chutney.
