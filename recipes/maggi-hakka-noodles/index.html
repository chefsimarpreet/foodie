Maggi Hakka Noodles

ChineseVegServes 2

Taking a cue from the Chinese-style Hakka noodles — Maggi Hakka Noodles is a dish which not only carries the goodness of fresh vegetables but the savour taste of Maggi noodles as well.

Ingredients

  • Masala Maggie 2 Packets and the tastemaker
  • Cabbage ¼ cup
  • Onions ¼ cup
  • Carrots ¼ cup
  • Bellpepper ¼ cup
  • Green onions 1 tbsp
  • Ginger 1 tsp
  • Green chillies 1/2 tbsp
  • Red Chilli Sauce 1 tsp
  • Schezwan Chutney 1 tsp
  • Soya sauce ¾ tsp
  • Hot and Sweet Tomato Ketchup 1 tsp
  • Oil 1 tbsp

Instructions

Mise-En-Place For Maggi Hakka Noodles

  1. Slice the vegetables thin lengthwise and keep aside in a bowl.
  2. Cook the maggi noodles in boiling water for two minutes and drain the noodles and throw away the excess water and keep the noodles in a bowl.

Method For Making The Maggi Hakka Noodles

  1. In a wok add oil, once the oil it hot add the ginger and the green chillies and cook for 30 seconds.
  2. Now add the onions and the carrots and cook on high flame till you see the onions turning a little translucent.
  3. Now add the cabbage, bell pepper and half of the green onion. Now cook for two or three minutes. The vegetables still need to have a little bite to them,
  4. Now add the sauce mix and mix well with the veggies and cook for 15-20 seconds.
  5. Add the cooked maggi noodles and mix well. If need be add a 2-3 spoons of water.
  6. Turn off the flame and remove in a plate.
  7. Garnish with the rest of the green onions and serve hot.
