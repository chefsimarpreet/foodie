Masala Chach

IndianVegServes 2

Masala chaas (also known as spiced buttermilk) is a summertime staple! It is so refreshing and timeless. All you need is a glass of chilled chach on a hot summer day, so cooling!!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 cups chilled water
  • salt to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper powder
  • ½ teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped green coriander
  • 10 finely chopped mint

Instructions

  1. Add yogurt, and water to a large bowl. Whisk well using a wire whisk so that there are no lumps. You can even use a blender to do the same.
  2. Add black pepper powder, salt, cumin powder, mint leaves, and coriander leaves, and mix well.
  3. Chill the chaach for a few hours before serving.
  4. If you don’t like mint and coriander coming into your mouth while sipping, strain the chach once the flavors have seeped in.
  5. You can also blend all the ingredients in a blender. By doing so, mint and coriander will get blended with the yogurt and they will not come in your mouth yet give the flavor.
