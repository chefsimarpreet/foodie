Paneer and Bell Pepper Grilled Sandwich

IndianVegServes 4

Paneer Capsicum Sandwich, a delicious combination of paneer, capsicum stuffed between toasted bread. Makes a great breakfast. Serve it with Cold Coffee, Banana Shake, tea or coffee.

Ingredients

  • 8 Whole Wheat Brown Bread
  • 200 grams Paneer, grated
  • 1 Red Bell Pepper (Capsicum) , finely chopped
  • 1 Green Chilli , finely chopped
  • 1.5 tbsp Garlic, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons Butter (Salted)
  • Salt , to taste

Instructions

  1. First, grate the paneer using a handy grater and place it in a big bowl.
  2. To this add the finely chopped capsicum, green chilli, garlic, salt and mix well.
  3. Take a non-stick pan, add 2 tbsp butter and put in the above mixture and cook till done.
  4. Now place a small portion of this mixture on a bread slice, spread it evenly and place another bread slice over it and press it gently to seal it.
  5. Similarly prepare the rest of the bread slices with the fillings.
  6. To make the grilled sandwiches, heat a sandwich maker, apply a little butter and place two sets of the sandwich prepared over it and toast it until it turns light brown. This will take around 2 minutes.
  7. Similarly, toast the other two sets of the sandwiches.
  8. Serve the Paneer Capsicum Sandwich Recipe along with Banana Shake for a quick breakfast or along with Masala Tea for an evening snacks.
