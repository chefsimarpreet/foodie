Poha

IndianVegServes 2

Healthy rice flake dish for mornings or snacks!

Ingredients

  • Poha (washed)
  • Onion – 3
  • Potatoes – 2 (deep fried)
  • Green Chilli – 3
  • Lemon Juice
  • Dhaniya
  • Kadi Pata
  • Mustard Seeds
  • Jeera Sabut
  • Peanuts (fried)
  • Desiccated Coconut
  • Sugar

Instructions

  1. Add mustard seeds in oil.
  2. When seeds start crackling, add few curry leaves and green chilli cut.
  3. Add Sabut Jeera.
  4. Add chopped onions. Make them light pink. Don’t overcook.
  5. Put on Sim.
  6. Add Haldi, Salt and Potatoes. Let haldi cook a little.
  7. Now add Poha (raw poha wetted a little).
  8. Sprinkle some water, Add a spoon of sugar.
  9. Turn of heat, cover the pan – and let it sit for 2 minutes, to absorb flavors.
  10. Now add Dhaniya and lemon.
