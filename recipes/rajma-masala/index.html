Rajma Masala

IndianVegServes 2

Rajma Masala consists of red kidney beans in a thick gravy with many Indian whole spices, and is usually served with rice.

Ingredients

For Pressure Cooking

  • 1 cup rajma
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 black cardamom
  • salt to taste
  • 4 cup water

Other Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp ghee / clarified butter
  • 1 tsp jeera
  • 1 inch cinnamon
  • 5 cloves
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 green chilli (slit)
  • 2 cup tomato crushed
  • ¼ tsp haldi
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp aamchur
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • salt to taste
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (crushed)
  • 2 tbsp green coriander (finely chopped)

Instructions

Soaking

  1. Rinse and soak the rajma (kidney beans) in enough water overnight or for 8 to 9 hours.
  2. Next day, discard the water and rinse the beans again in fresh water for a couple of times.
  3. Drain all the water.

Pressure Cooking

  1. In a 3 litre pressure cooker, take both the soaked rajma and water.
  2. Pressure cook on a medium to high flame for 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. When the pressure settles down on its own, open the lid.
  4. Check if the rajma beans are cooked or not by taking a bite or pressing a few beans.
  5. If the beans are cooked well set aside or drain the beans. Reserve the stock (water) in which the beans were cooked.
  6. If the rajma are not cooked completely, then pressure cook again adding some water if required for some minutes.

Making Masala

  1. In a large kadai heat 1 tbsp ghee and saute 1 tsp cumin, 1 inch cinnamon and 5 cloves.
  2. add in chopped onion, ginger garlic paste, green chilli and saute until the onions turn golden brown. now add crushed tomato pulp. cover and cook for 10 minutes, or until the oil is separated.
  3. further keeping the flame on low add haldi, chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, aamchur, garam masala and Salt to taste. saute until the spices turn aromatic.
  4. add in cooked rajma and mix well.
  5. cover and simmer for 15 minutes or until the curry thickens.
  6. now add kasuri methi, green coriander and mix well.
  7. Enjoy rajma with hot boiled rice and raita.
