Chicken Dum Biryani

IndianNon-vegServes 2

Chicken Biryani is a savory chicken and rice dish that includes layers of chicken, rice, and aromatics that are steamed together. The bottom layer of rice absorbs all the chicken juices as it cooks, giving it a tender texture and rich flavor, while the top layer of rice turns out white and fluffy.

Ingredients

  • Chicken Boneless – 350 gms
  • Boiled Basmati Rice – 300 gms
  • Garam Masala Powder – 20 gms
  • Ghee – 30 ml

For Marination

  • Yogurt (Curd) beaten – 60 gms
  • Ginger Garlic Paste – 30 gms
  • Salt – to taste
  • Mint Leaves – 1 gm
  • Thinly Sliced Onions (Deep fried to Golden Brown)
  • Coriander leaves – 1 tbsp
  • Red Chilli Pwdr – 7 gms

For Garnishing (Layering)

  • Saffron – 2 dashes
  • Fried Onion – 2 tbsp
  • Mint Leaves – 1 gms
  • Kewra – 2 drops
  • Biryani Essence – 2 drops
  • Milk
  • Boiled Egg cut in half

Instructions

  1. To prepare this Mughlai recipe, take a large bowl mix chicken with yoghurt (curd), fried onion, red chilli powder, salt, coriander leaves, mint leaves, ginger and garlic paste. Mix all the ingredients well and let the chicken marinate for about 2 hours. When the chicken is marinated, add garam masala in it.
  2. Once the chicken is marinated, melt ghee in a handi over medium flame. When ghee is melted, add marinated chicken in it and fry for 2-3 minutes. Then, add water in it and cook the chicken on low-medium flame. Put the lid on vessel once the chicken is cooked.
  3. Now, add 80 per cent cooked basmati rice in it. Above the rice layer, add brown or fried onion, ghee, milk, kewra, biryani essence and saffron water (saffron soaked in 2 tbsp water) and a little water. On the top add Boiled Egg cut in half lengthwise.
  4. Knead a quick dough with wheat flour and seal the handi with this dough, and then cover with a lid. Seal the handi and let the biryani stay on medium fire for 20 minutes. Serve it hot with raita.
