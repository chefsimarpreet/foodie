Palak Pakoda

IndianVegServes 3

Palak pakoda are made with fresh spinach leaves, besan, spices and herbs. These spinach pakora make for a great tea-time snack & can be served with Indian masala chai or with a chutney.

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch Spinach Leaves
  • 1 cup Besan
  • ½ tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Ginger Paste
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Coriander (Finely Chopped)
  • 1 tsp Green chilli (Finely Chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 tbsp Chaat Masala

Instructions

  1. Mix besan, red chilli powder, turmeric powder, ginger paste, fresh coriander, green chilli, baking soda, lemon juice and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add water and make a batter.
  3. The batter should be just thin enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  4. Heat oil for frying the pakora in a pan.
  5. Once the oil is hot, simmer the heat to medium low.
  6. Dip the spinach leaves in the batter and drop them in the hot oil.
  7. Deep fry until golden brown from both the sides.
  8. Drain on a tissue lined plate.
  9. Fry all the pakora in the same manner.
  10. Sprinkle chaat masala as soon as the pakoras are out from oil and toss well.
  11. Serve hot with green coriander chutney.
