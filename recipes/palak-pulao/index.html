Palak Pulao

IndianVegServes 2

Palak rice or palak pulao is a healthy, delicious and colorful dish made of spinach, spices & rice. It can be served without any side or with a salad or raita. Palak rice is quick to make for a meal and also goes well in lunch box.

Ingredients

  • Palak – 1 bunch
  • Basmati Rice – 1 cup (cooked to 80%)
  • Onions – 2 Medium
  • Peas – 1 cup
  • Green Chilli – 2
  • Salt – To Taste
  • Ghee – 1 tbsp

Instructions

  1. In a wok , take a tsp of ghee.
  2. Add chopped onions and green chilli, fry till they turn translucent.
  3. Time to add finely chopped palak leaves and fry for a minute till they shrink and change its colour
  4. Once the palak leaves are cooked, add peas and fry till the peas are cooked.
  5. Finally, add cooked rice and salt to taste.
  6. Mix well without breaking rice grains. cook for a minute.
  7. Serve palak pulao with raita.
