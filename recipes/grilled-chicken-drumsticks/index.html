Grilled Chicken Drumsticks

IndianNon-vegServes 2

Grilled Chicken Drumsticks is a quick meal with tender juicy chicken in a flavorful marinade

Ingredients

  • 6 Pcs of Chicken legs
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons salt
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons Black Pepper
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 1 Tsp Ginger Paste
  • 1 Bunch Coriander Leaves
  • 1 cup Fresh Yogurt
  • 3 Green Chillies

Instructions

  1. Coat chicken pieces in salt, Pepper, garlic powder, ginger paste and the paste of Coriander Leaves with yogurt and green chillies.
  2. Cook the marinated chicken in a wok for 15 minutes.
  3. Preheat the Air Fryer at 190 degree Celsius for 8 minutes.
  4. Transfer the chicken in the air fryer until golden brown. Keep brushing with butter to avoid drying and work in batches as needed.
