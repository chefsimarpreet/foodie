TANDOORI, In Indian cookery, particularly in the Punjab, a method of cooking in a clay oven known as Tandoor. Breads, Chicken, meat or fish are cooked in a Tandoor, which makes them crisp, well browned and gives the smokey flavor. The resulting food is known as Tandoori.
